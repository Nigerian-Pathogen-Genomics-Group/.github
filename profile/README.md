# Nigerian Pathogen Genomics Group (NPGG) 🇳🇬

> Advancing pathogen genomics, parasite genomics, microbiome research, and bioinformatics through collaborative science, reproducible workflows, and capacity building.

## About Us

**Nigerian Pathogen Genomics Group (NPGG)** is a collaborative research community bringing together researchers, microbiologists, parasitologists, bioinformaticians, epidemiologists, and other scientists interested in understanding pathogens, microbial communities, and their relationships with human, animal, and environmental health.

Our work brings together laboratory science, genomics, microbiome research, and computational biology to investigate biological questions relevant to infectious diseases, public health, and microbial ecology.

We aim to strengthen genomics and bioinformatics research capacity in Nigeria through collaborative projects, practical training, open science, and the development of reproducible computational approaches.

## Our Research Areas

 **Pathogen Genomics:** Genome characterization, comparative genomics, molecular epidemiology, and evolutionary analysis of infectious agents.
 **Parasite Genomics and Molecular Parasitology:** Genomic and molecular investigation of parasites, their diversity, evolution, transmission, and relationships with their hosts.
 **Microbiome Research:** Computational analysis of microbial communities associated with human health, disease, animals, and the environment.
 **Microbial Ecology and Environmental Genomics:** Exploring microbial diversity, community structure, and interactions across different environments.
 **Infectious Disease Research and Genomic Surveillance:** Applying genomic and bioinformatics approaches to investigate infectious diseases and support public health surveillance.
 **Antimicrobial Resistance (AMR):** Investigating the genetic basis, distribution, and evolution of antimicrobial resistance in relevant microbial populations.
 **Computational Biology, Data Science, and Machine Learning:** Applying statistical analysis, bioinformatics, and machine learning to genomic, microbiome, and other biological datasets.

## What We Do

* Conduct collaborative research in pathogen genomics, parasite genomics, microbiome science, and microbial ecology.
* Analyse genomic and microbiome datasets using reproducible computational methods.
* Develop, document, and share open-source bioinformatics workflows.
* Organize practical training in bioinformatics, genomics, and computational biology.
* Support research collaborations, scientific writing, and manuscript development.
* Promote open science, data sharing, and transparent, reproducible research.
* Build connections among researchers, universities, laboratories, sequencing facilities, and public health institutions.

## Training and Capacity Building

We support researchers and students in developing practical skills for modern biological research, including:

* Linux and command-line computing
* R and Python for biological data analysis
* Nextflow and the nf-core ecosystem
* Reproducible and scalable bioinformatics workflows
* Pathogen and parasite genome analysis
* Microbiome and metagenomic data analysis
* Phylogenetics, comparative genomics, and genomic epidemiology
* Statistical analysis and machine learning for biological data
* Git, GitHub, and collaborative open-source research

Nextflow is one of the workflow technologies we use and teach as part of our broader commitment to reproducible and scalable computational research. Our training activities are designed to support a range of research applications and are not limited to a single tool or biological discipline.

## Our Mission

To advance pathogen and parasite genomics, microbiome research, and computational biology in Nigeria by building research capacity, fostering scientific collaboration, and promoting reproducible approaches to biological and public health challenges.
